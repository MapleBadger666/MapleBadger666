# Mathematics and research software

I study mathematics at the University of British Columbia. I'm interested in statistical modeling and applied mathematics, especially their use in machine learning and quantitative research. My approach to research software engineering emphasizes transparent assumptions, careful evaluation, and reproducible results.

## Selected projects

- [**Bankiller Quant Research Platform**](https://github.com/MapleBadger666/bankiller-quant-research-platform) uses deterministic synthetic equity data to demonstrate cross-sectional factor evaluation with Spearman Rank IC, forward returns, and quantile diagnostics. A [separate synthetic point-in-time example](https://github.com/MapleBadger666/bankiller-quant-research-platform/blob/main/docs/methodology.md#point-in-time-constraints) illustrates how delayed observations and revisions become visible and when outcome labels are available for training or evaluation.
- [**Smartphone Battery Modeling · COMAP MCM 2026**](https://github.com/MapleBadger666/mcm-battery-modeling) is a team project on smartphone discharge using coupled state-of-charge and temperature ODEs, calibration against discharge records, and exploratory modeling of aging proxies.
